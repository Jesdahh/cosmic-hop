HOW TO PLAY

THE GOAL

  • Explore 3 star systems and land on every world (★ counter on the MAP button).
  • Discover alien life, mine ore, finish missions, and upgrade your gear.
  • Earn 🪙 coins and buy new rockets & astronaut suits.

KEYBOARD

  • WASD fly / walk · MOUSE steer · SHIFT boost / run
  • L land / take off · E exit ship, board, use
  • SPACE jump (hold in air = jetpack)
  • F or click: mine · C scan · P photo mode · T read treasure map
  • G eat · H medkit · K shop
  • U tech · B build · M map · J log · I this help

FRONTIER LIFE

  • POINTS OF INTEREST: crashed ships, derelict probes, sealed vaults (find 3 glyph stones, then enter the code), and stranded survivors. Press E near them.
  • TREASURE MAPS drop from probes & vaults. Press T on a planet to reveal a buried cache waypoint.
  • WILDLIFE: walk up to a creature and press E with fruit to tame it. Tamed creatures follow you. Scan every species on a world for a catalogue reward.
  • PHOTO MODE: P hides the HUD, ENTER captures. Photograph creatures for Star Cartographer contracts.
  • FACTIONS: rescues, salvage, photos and discoveries earn reputation and daily / weekly contracts. Claim them in LOG → FACTIONS.
  • BASES: press E at an outpost for storage, farms and teleporters. Visit your bases from the MAP.
  • STORM RUSH: when a solar storm hits, glowing storm crystals erupt nearby. Mine them before the storm passes!
  • ALIEN RAIDERS: raider packs hunt you while you fly between worlds, arriving after a short warning. Hold F (or FIRE on phone) — your laser auto-locks the nearest raider. Their hits also chew through hull HP. Destroy them for coins, ore, Guild rep and contracts, or boost away until they jump out. Systems 3–4 send alien DREADNOUGHTS — huge volley-firing capital ships guarded by escorts, with heavy loot.
  • CIVILIZED SYSTEMS 5–7: Khepri, Vesk and the Weaving host living cities with alien citizens. Land and they will defend their world — citizens zap you on foot and armed orbital defense platforms open fire on your ship at HOSTILE alert. Fight back with your laser (F / FIRE): destroy citizens and platforms for loot, but every kill raises their alert. Hail the colony beacon [E] to pay tribute and stand down, or clear their missions to earn exclusive rocket & suit skins.
  • UNKNOWN VESSELS: friendly alien ships from other star systems cruise the spacelanes. Fly close and press E (or tap HAIL) to talk — first contact with each species pays credits, Cartographer rep and codex data.

PHONE & TABLET

  • Left side: drag the joystick to fly or walk.
  • Right side: swipe to look and steer.
  • Big buttons: BOOST/RUN, LAND, EXIT, MINE, SCAN.
  • Tap 🍎 to eat, 💊 for a medkit, 🪙 for the shop.
  • Turn your phone sideways for a wider view. ⛶ = full screen.

STAYING ALIVE

  • HP bar = health. 🍗 bar = food. Food slowly runs out; when it's empty you lose health.
  • Food grows on most worlds. Walk around and look for glowing 🍓 berries & 🍐 star fruit on the ground near you — you'll also see a "FOOD NEARBY" hint.
  • Lava worlds (CINDER, TARTARUS) and frozen GLACIA have no food — bring 🥫 rations from the shop.
  • Crashing your ship too fast, lava, and solar storms hurt. Rest in your landed ship to heal faster.
  • HP 0 = knocked out: drones bring you back to your ship for a 10% coin fee.

EARNING COINS

  • Mining ore: 1–6 🪙 each · coin trails in space & coins on the ground: 5 🪙
  • New discovery 15 🪙 · new world 25 🪙 · ruin puzzle 60 🪙 · mission 100 🪙
  • Sell spare ore in the shop. Spend coins on rockets, suits, rations & medkits.

GETTING FURTHER

  • TECH: upgrade your mining tool, suit and hyperdrive with ore.
  • MAP: pick a planet to fly there, or warp to a new star system (needs hyperdrive upgrades).
  • BUILD: craft shield/jet cells, outposts and a rover.
  • You can swim in water and walk across the red bridges. The game saves automatically.
