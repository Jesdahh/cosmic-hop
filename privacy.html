Privacy Policy

Cosmic Hop: Galactic Frontier — last updated 18 November 2026

Short version: the game has no accounts, no analytics and no ad trackers. Your progress is stored only on your own device. When you play multiplayer, your pilot name and ship position are sent directly to the other players in your lobby, never to a game server. The only third parties involved are the hosting, code-delivery and connection services listed below.

1. Who we are

“Cosmic Hop: Galactic Frontier” (the “Game”) is developed by the Cosmic Hop team and published at cosmic-hop.pages.dev. You can reach us through the project page at github.com/Jesdahh/cosmic-hop.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves data in your browser’s or app’s local storage. This data stays on your device and is not uploaded anywhere:

  • cosmic_hop_gf_save — your game progress (planets visited, resources, upgrades, missions, unlocks).
  • cosmic_hop_mp_name — the pilot name you type for multiplayer.
  • cosmic_hop_turn — an optional connection setting you can enter yourself.

You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the site’s data in your browser settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Multiplayer

Multiplayer lobbies are peer-to-peer. When you join a lobby, the following is shared with the other players in that lobby only:

  • the pilot name you choose;
  • your ship and astronaut appearance, position, orientation and game state (system, planet, flight/walking mode);
  • chat messages and emotes you send, delivered to the players in the lobby.

This information is not stored on any game server and is not visible to anyone outside your lobby. To introduce players to each other, encrypted connection information passes through public signalling relays; the Game does not send your progress or personal details through them. When a direct connection between players is not possible, the connection is carried by a TURN relay (see section 5); the relay forwards encrypted traffic and cannot read it.

4. What we do not collect

  • No accounts, email addresses or passwords.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising identifiers or ad trackers in the web version.
  • No access to your contacts, files, camera, microphone or location.

5. Third-party services

The Game relies on these services, which may process technical data such as your IP address as part of normal operation:

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. The Game does not control what they log.

6. Cookies

The Game does not set cookies. It uses local storage only for the settings listed in section 2.

7. Advertising

The current version of the Game shows no advertising. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before those ads go live.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are under 13, please play with a parent or guardian.

9. Your choices

  • You can change your multiplayer name at any time from the multiplayer panel.
  • You can leave a lobby at any time; other players then stop receiving your ship data.
  • You can export your save from the Journal before clearing data, and import it later.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new “last updated” date. Significant changes will also be announced in the Game.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project page: github.com/Jesdahh/cosmic-hop.
